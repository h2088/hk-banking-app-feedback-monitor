## Preliminary themes to test, not findings

1. Login / authentication / app launch stability
2. Transaction authorization and payment flows
3. Customer support / in-app chat
4. Transfer or repayment status and timing
5. Navigation and usability

These are candidate labels based on a preliminary scan of visible public comments. They must be validated against collected records and must not be presented as six-month frequency or trend results.

## Risks and decisions

- Search-engine snippets do not constitute a complete review dataset.
- Current store ratings/review counts are snapshots, not six-month measurements.
- Full historical coverage has not yet been established.
- Social media should be reported as sampled public discussion, not as exhaustive monitoring, unless coverage can be proven.
- No sentiment percentage, monthly trend, or app-level conclusion should be published until the underlying records are collected and quality-checked.
