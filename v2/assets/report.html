How to read this report

Window, sources and collection limits

SC and HSBC reuse the original 19 October 2026 snapshots. BOCHK was collected on 22 October and filtered to the same 19 April–19 October review window. A later collection cannot reconstruct reviews that were deleted or edited after 19 October. Captured English counts do not establish complete coverage of all English reviews.

BOCHK Google Play returned 600 unique reviews from three pages; collection stopped after two pages entirely before the window. Apple returned 500 reviews from pages 1–10; the page-11 boundary request returned HTTP 400. Accepted pages remain usable; the public feed is bounded. Raw responses, errors, exclusions and coverage dates are saved with the project.

Sentiment and topic methods

The source comment body is the text field. Apple titles supplement keyword matching and search. Developer replies are excluded. Customer text is preserved verbatim and is not translated.

Rating sentiment uses 1–2 stars = negative, 3 = neutral and 4–5 = positive. It is a rating proxy, not a claim that the text expresses the same sentiment. Eleven transparent keyword themes are multi-label mentions, not confirmed complaints or defect incidence. Broad terms such as service, account, notification, ads and FX can overmatch. Themes are a triage aid.

English selection uses script and short-word checks plus langdetect 1.0.9 (fixed seed 0, threshold 0.80), with documented exact-text agent corrections. Existing SC/HSBC decisions are retained. Language detection and theme matching are imperfect. The stratified reading check was performed by a Codex agent; it is not an independent human accuracy evaluation.

Comparisons, evidence and interpretation

Each denominator is specific to one bank and one store. Mean ratings here use the collected English reviews in this window, rather than a store's headline lifetime rating. Theme mention shares divide by the bank/store sample; theme low-star shares in the data tables divide by theme mentions. Monthly shares use monthly denominators. Empty groups show N/A.

Different IDs with repeated wording remain separate. Counts refer to review records, not distinct customers. No significance, customer-wide satisfaction, causal explanation, incident severity or business loss is inferred. Validate reported journeys with support tickets and telemetry before deciding a fix.

Google source links contain the review ID. Apple links generally open the app's review listing; locate the exact title, text, rating and date. Live listings can change after collection. Full raw evidence and the typed Excel workbook are provided separately in the V2 project folder.
