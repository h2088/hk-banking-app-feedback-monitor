# Checkpoint D — Customer Feedback Monitoring and Benchmark Analysis

**Checked on:** 2026-10-19  
**Status:** First analysis pass complete; suitable for descriptive reporting with the limitations below  
**Scope:** Monitor and report customer feedback comments only  
**Primary app:** SC Mobile Hong Kong  
**Benchmark:** HSBC HK Mobile Banking  
**Input:** [311-row English six-month analysis file](data/merged_2026-10-19/master_english_six_months.csv)

## 1. Scope and the text field

Yes. The **text** field is the main customer-feedback comment body:

- Google Play text is the public review body returned by the source.
- Apple App Store has both title and text. The original fields remain separate. For searching and rule-based topic matching only, this analysis uses title plus text as comment_text_for_analysis; it does not replace the original text.
- rating is the user-provided star rating and the primary comparable satisfaction signal.
- review_time_hk is used for monthly feedback monitoring.
- app_key distinguishes SC from HSBC.

All reported results here aggregate Google Play and Apple App Store as requested. Platform is retained in the row-level data only for evidence tracing. This analysis does not use social posts, installation counts, active users, store-level aggregate ratings, display names, or developer replies as customer-comment feedback. It analyzes review records, not unique customers.

## 6. Monitoring priorities

1. **App stability and loading:** Track crash, freeze, white-screen, loading and connection wording separately from general negative sentiment. SC has 28 matched comments (28.57%) versus HSBC’s 20 (9.39%); 26 of SC’s 28 are 1–2 stars.
2. **Login and authentication:** Monitor verification, approval, notification, OTP and login wording as a separate journey. SC has 27 matched comments (27.55%) versus HSBC’s 19 (8.92%); 24 of SC’s 27 are 1–2 stars.
3. **Transfers, payments and cards:** Track transfer-page loading, payment/transaction failures and card approval wording. This is the most-mentioned SC theme (31 comments, 31.63%) versus 35 HSBC comments (16.43%).
4. **Usability and navigation:** Continue monitoring layout, menus, fonts and flow complexity. It is common for both apps (SC 19.39%; HSBC 17.37%).
5. **Support and feature requests:** Report support/service and explicit feature-request comments separately; do not mix them into failure rates.

Each monitoring period should show volume, 1–2-star share, mean rating, top theme mentions, and at least one source-linked example. Keep the denominator visible.

## 7. Method and validation

- Star sentiment is deterministic: 1–2 negative, 3 neutral, 4–5 positive. It is a monitoring convention, not a psychological diagnosis.
- Text polarity is a supplemental phrase lexicon. It is reported separately because short comments, negation, sarcasm and mixed statements can fool keyword rules.
- A 10-row spot-check reviewed one newest-first row for each available app/rating stratum. Star labels were plausible for 10/10; broad themes were plausible for 10/10. Two comments showed text/rating disagreement and were retained rather than silently corrected.
- 105 of 311 review bodies (`text` only, Checkpoint C's QA definition) have four or fewer English words. The analysis counts Apple `title + text`, so its `short_text_for_theme_review` flags total 83 (SC 17, HSBC 66). These are different text-length definitions, not removed records. They remain valid for volume and rating monitoring but provide limited evidence for detailed product themes.
- Five normalized repeated-text groups cover 20 rows (good, excellent, nice, easy to use, great apps). They have distinct review IDs and were retained.
- The analysis uses all 311 English records. It does not rebalance HSBC or infer missing reviews.

## 8. Limitations

- Apple public RSS was capped at 500 records per app; Google results are the returned public endpoint sample for the requested Hong Kong listing parameters. Neither is a complete platform history.
- Review volume is not usage-adjusted. A comment share is not a rate among all customers.
- The latest observed English record is before the 2026-10-19 as-of date; the as-of date is an upper bound, not an observed-review claim.
- Overall aggregation follows the requested no-platform-split presentation. Unequal platform composition and sample sizes make this descriptive benchmarking, not a controlled experiment.
- Analysis is English-only. Traditional Chinese and other-language feedback require a separate multilingual method.
- Theme labels are rule-based and do not prove root cause, severity, business impact or statistical significance.

## 9. Checkpoint D handoff

**Decision:** the first descriptive customer-feedback analysis is complete. The core findings are:

1. SC’s feedback profile is more negative than HSBC’s benchmark: mean rating 2.2551 versus 3.2817, and 1–2-star share 66.33% versus 40.38%.
2. SC feedback is more associated with stability/performance, login/authentication and transfer/payment wording than HSBC feedback.
3. The data supports monitoring and reporting comments, but not claims of complete review coverage, unique-customer rates, causality or statistically significant product superiority.

Move to Checkpoint E only after accepting the conservative taxonomy and validation sample.
